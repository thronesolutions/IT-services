Maximizing Microsoft 365: Tips for South Yorkshire's Remote Workforce

Published on October 21, 2026 | By Throne Solutions

The shift to remote and hybrid work is no longer a temporary trend; it is a permanent fixture of the modern business landscape. For businesses across South Yorkshire, Microsoft 365 (M365) has become the central hub for collaboration, communication, and data storage. However, many organizations are only scratching the surface of what M365 can do.

Are you using M365 simply as a digital version of Word and Outlook, or are you leveraging its full suite of productivity and security tools?

Security First: It Starts with Identity

In a remote environment, your employees are accessing company data from various locations and devices. This makes identity management critical. Unfortunately, relying on simple passwords is no longer safe.

If you haven't already, you must enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA). This single step blocks a vast majority of automated cyberattacks. Beyond MFA, you should implement Conditional Access policies. This allows your IT team to set rules, such as requiring additional verification if an employee tries to log in from an unrecognized device or a foreign country.

Streamlining Collaboration with Teams and SharePoint

Microsoft Teams is a powerful tool, but without proper governance, it can quickly become chaotic. It is essential to establish clear rules for how Teams channels and SharePoint sites are created and structured.

Encourage your team to use the built-in co-authoring features in real-time. Instead of emailing documents back and forth, use OneDrive or SharePoint to collaborate on a single version of a file. This eliminates version control issues and ensures that everyone is always working on the latest document.

Automate the Mundane with Power Automate

One of the most underutilized features of M365 is Power Automate. It allows you to create automated workflows between your favorite apps and services. For example, you can set up a workflow to automatically save email attachments to a specific OneDrive folder, or to send a notification to your team when a new file is uploaded.

By automating these small, repetitive tasks, you can give your employees hours of their time back each week. If you need help setting up advanced workflows, consulting with a specialized Managed IT provider can save you significant time and frustration.

Don't Leave Your Data Vulnerable

Finally, ensure that data loss prevention (DLP) policies are in place. M365 allows you to restrict the sharing of sensitive information (like credit card numbers or patient data) outside your organization.

If your business in South Yorkshire is ready to take full control of its digital workspace and secure its remote workforce, conducting a comprehensive Microsoft 365 audit is the perfect first step.
